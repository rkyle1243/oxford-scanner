One-time setup

Transcribing happens entirely in your browser, but OpenMHz’s audio host won’t let a web page read its files — only play them. A tiny proxy of your own fixes that. It stores nothing and only ever fetches from openmhz.com.
  1. Open Cloudflare Workers and create a Worker (free plan is plenty).
  2. Paste in the contents of proxy/worker.js from this repo, then deploy.
  3. Paste the Worker URL below.
Transcripts are machine-generated from noisy trunked audio and get words wrong — names, streets and plate readbacks especially. Treat them as a way to find a call, then listen to it. They are not a record of what was said. Everything stays in this browser; the only things leaving it are model weights from a CDN and audio requests through your own proxy.
